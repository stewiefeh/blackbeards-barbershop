the shop

One chair. One barber. No cattle drive.

This is a real barbershop with a pirate’s soul. Craig runs it solo, takes his time, and finishes every fade and custom design like it’s going on a poster. Nobody gets herded through here like cattle — plan to spend an hour and leave looking like you should.

Beards get the full treatment too: oils, warm towels, and a steady hand. It’s the kind of place people drive across town for and then tell their friends about.

the manifest

What’s on the ledger

  • ⚔Classic cuts — the standards, done properly, scissor and clipper work included
  • ⚔Fades & custom designs — freehand work with real attention to detail, down to the etched lines
  • ⚔Beard grooming — shaping, oils, and warm towels, the slow way
  • ⚔Kids’ cuts — patient with first-timers and wiggly first mates alike

A note from the crew: cards are taken, but cash is preferred. Come early — the chair fills up fast.
